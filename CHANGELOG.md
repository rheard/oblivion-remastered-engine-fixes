# Changelog

## Unreleased

- Stopping a magic effect on an object that was just appearing or going away crashed the game.

## 1.0.0

The first release, with six fixes:

- Picking up an item a mod placed in the world crashed the game.
- Loading a save could crash after removing a mod that casts spells.
- An item that removes itself in its own script crashed the game.
- Text from mods showed "[NL]" in front.
- Spells a mod removed stayed listed in the open magic menu.
- Stealing could add a fraction of a gold to your bounty.
